A little note from my heart

Dear Jyoti ❤️

Jyoti,

Sometimes a simple “I'm sorry” feels too small for everything sitting inside the heart.

I know I cannot go back and change what happened. I cannot erase a moment, take back a word, or pretend that everything was perfect.

“I'm genuinely sorry.” ❤️

I know that an apology does not automatically fix everything. And I don't expect a few words on a screen to magically make everything okay.

I'm not asking you to forget what happened. I'm not asking you to pretend that you weren't hurt.

I only want you to know that I have thought about it, I have understood things I didn't understand before, and I want to learn from my mistakes.

My promises ❤️
👂

I will listen.

Your feelings deserve to be heard and understood.

🌷

I will respect.

Your space, boundaries, choices and feelings.

💬

I will communicate.

I will choose honest conversation over assumptions.

❤️

I will show it.

My actions should speak louder than my promises.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every silly moment, every beautiful memory and every little thing that became special without us even realizing it.

Maybe I didn't always understand the value of those moments while they were happening. But I understand it now.

💌

You don't have to forgive me just because I made this page.

You don't have to forget anything.

Take whatever time your heart needs.

I only hope that someday, when you look back at this, you can see that my apology was genuine.

If I could change one thing, I would go back and handle those moments with more patience, more understanding and more care.

Since I cannot change yesterday, all I can do is try to make tomorrow better.

❤️

I'm sorry, Jyoti.

Truly.

And thank you for being such a beautiful part of my memories.

With a sincere heart,
Someone who truly cares ❤️
